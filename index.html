Jordi Laguarta Soler

PhD candidate, MIT EECS · Earth Intelligence Lab

I am a fifth‑year MIT PhD student in EECS, specialising in AI, and part of the Earth Intelligence Lab supervised by Dr. Sherrie Wang.

I build multimodal ML systems end to end for domains where expert labels are scarce: self‑supervised foundation models, foundation models as annotators, explainable predictions, and national‑scale inference evaluated under distribution shift. At MIT, I have applied them to high‑stakes problems in health and food security: screening for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's from coughs and speech, and high‑resolution mapping of smallholder crops at country scale. At Microsoft Research, I built a longitudinal multimodal satellite foundation model.

I lead research on evaluation science: whether geospatial benchmarks can reliably tell foundation models apart. I have mentored student researchers for over three years.

Work

  • Model-generated supervision · MIT · 2023–⁠now · in preparation

    A 10 m crop map of India, supervised by a vision-language model

    I prompt a VLM to label street‑level images, then distil it into a lightweight classifier on foundation‑model embeddings that maps crops at national scale.

    Skills: VLM-as-annotator, Cross-modal distillation, Foundation-model embeddings, Country-scale inference

  • Audio ML for health · MIT Auto-ID Lab · 2020–⁠21 · 700+ citations

    Acoustic biomarkers for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's screening

    Acoustic-biomarker encoders pretrained on separate audio tasks transfer to COVID‑19 screening from coughs and Alzheimer's screening from speech, with per-patient saliency maps. I was first author on both papers.

    Skills: Audio representation learning, Transfer learning, Explainability, Disease screening

  • Foundation models · Microsoft Research · 2024

    A multimodal foundation model for satellite time series

    I designed the encoder architecture, built the data‑streaming pipeline and ran distributed multi‑GPU pretraining on radar, optical and land‑cover time series.

    Skills: Self-supervised pretraining, Cross-modal prediction, Distributed multi-GPU training, High-throughput data streaming

  • Benchmark reliability · MIT · 2026–⁠now · in progress

    Which Earth-observation benchmarks can tell foundation models apart?

    I lead an analysis of published geospatial foundation-model results that tests, benchmark by benchmark, whether the gaps between models exceed seed noise.

    Skills: Evaluation science, Statistical model comparison, Seed variance, Foundation models

  • Zero-shot VLM supervision · MIT · AAAI 2024

    A national crop map of Thailand from Street View and zero-shot VLM labels

    I replaced expert annotation with zero-shot GPT‑4V labels as the training signal, and the map comes close to the expert‑supervised one.

    Skills: Zero-shot VLM labelling, Pseudo-labelling, Street-to-satellite transfer, Satellite time series
