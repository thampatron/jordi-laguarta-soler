Jordi Laguarta Soler

PhD candidate, MIT EECS · Earth Intelligence Lab

I build multimodal ML systems end to end for domains where expert labels are scarce: foundation models as annotators, self-supervised pretraining, explainable predictions, and national-scale inference evaluated under distribution shift. At MIT and Microsoft Research, I have applied them to high‑stakes problems in health and food security: screening for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's from coughs and speech, and mapping crops across entire countries.

I lead research on evaluation science: whether geospatial benchmarks can reliably tell foundation models apart. I have mentored student researchers for over three years.

    Acoustic-biomarker encoders pretrained on separate audio tasks transfer to COVID‑19 screening from coughs and Alzheimer's screening from speech, with per-patient saliency maps. First author on both papers.

    I designed the architecture, built the data-streaming pipeline and ran distributed multi-GPU pretraining on radar, optical and land-cover time series.

  • Benchmark reliability · MIT · 2026–now · draft

    Which Earth-observation benchmarks can tell foundation models apart?

    I lead an analysis of published geospatial foundation-model results that asks, benchmark by benchmark, whether the gaps between models exceed seed noise.

    Skills: Evaluation science · Statistical model comparison · Seed variance · Foundation models

  • Zero-shot VLM supervision · MIT · AAAI 2024

    A national crop map of Thailand from Street View and zero-shot VLM labels

    Zero-shot GPT‑4V labels replace expert annotation as the training signal, and the map comes close to the expert-supervised one.
