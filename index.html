Jordi Laguarta Soler

PhD candidate, MIT EECS · Earth Intelligence Lab

I build multimodal ML systems end to end for domains where expert labels are scarce: foundation models as annotators, self-supervised pretraining, explainable predictions, and national-scale inference evaluated under distribution shift. At MIT and Microsoft Research, I have applied them to high-stakes problems in health and food security: screening for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's from coughs and speech, and mapping crops across entire countries. I lead research on evaluation science: whether geospatial foundation-model benchmarks detect real differences between models or just seed noise. I also mentor student researchers.

Selected work

All work →
  • Model-generated supervision · MIT · 2023–now · in preparation

    A 10 m crop map of India, supervised by a vision-language model

    Gemini 3.1 Pro annotates street-level imagery from every agricultural district; the labels supervise a lightweight classifier on frozen AlphaEarth embeddings, which runs national inference in under four hours.

  • Acoustic biomarkers · MIT Auto-ID Lab · 2020–21 · 715 citations

    Acoustic biomarkers for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's screening

    Acoustic-biomarker feature extractors transferred to COVID‑19 detection from forced coughs and Alzheimer's detection from spontaneous speech, with per-patient saliency maps. First author on both papers.

  • Foundation models · Microsoft Research · 2024

    A multimodal foundation model for satellite time series

    Self-supervised pretraining on 20 TB of radar, optical and land-cover time series, with masked months of one sensor predicted from the others. I designed the architecture and ran the distributed training.

  • Benchmark reliability · MIT · 2026–now · draft

    Which Earth-observation benchmarks can tell foundation models apart?

    I lead a meta-analysis of published geospatial foundation-model results that asks, benchmark by benchmark, whether the gaps between models exceed seed variance.

  • Cross-view supervision · MIT · AAAI 2024

    Mapping smallholder crop types in Thailand from Street View

    A street-view CNN produces 81,000 field labels that supervise a national 10 m satellite map. In 2023, replacing expert annotation with GPT‑4V zero-shot labels cost one point of map accuracy (92% vs. 93%).
