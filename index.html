Jordi Laguarta Soler

PhD candidate, MIT EECS · Earth Intelligence Lab

I build multimodal ML systems end to end for domains where expert labels are scarce: foundation models as annotators, self-supervised pretraining, explainable predictions, and national-scale inference evaluated under distribution shift. At MIT and Microsoft Research, I have applied them to high‑stakes problems in health and food security: screening for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's from coughs and speech, and mapping crops across entire countries.

I lead research on evaluation science: whether geospatial foundation-model benchmarks resolve real differences between models beyond seed variance. I have mentored student researchers for over three years.

Work

  • Model-generated supervision · MIT · 2023–now · in preparation

    A 10 m crop map of India, supervised by a vision-language model

    A vision-language model labels street-level imagery at national scale, and those labels train a lightweight classifier on foundation-model embeddings that maps crops across the whole country.

    Skills: VLM-as-annotator · Model-generated supervision · Foundation-model embeddings · Country-scale inference

  • Acoustic biomarkers · MIT Auto-ID Lab · 2020–21 · 700+ citations

    Acoustic biomarkers for COVID‑19 and Alzheimer's screening

    Pretrained acoustic-biomarker encoders transferred to screening for COVID‑19 from coughs and Alzheimer's from speech, with explainable per-patient outputs. First author on both papers.

    Skills: Audio representation learning · Transfer learning · Explainability · Health AI

  • Foundation models · Microsoft Research · 2024

    A multimodal foundation model for satellite time series

    I designed and pretrained a multimodal foundation model for satellite time series, covering the architecture, distributed training and the data pipeline.

    Skills: Self-supervised pretraining · Multimodal foundation models · Distributed multi-GPU training · High-throughput data pipelines

  • Benchmark reliability · MIT · 2026–now · draft

    Which Earth-observation benchmarks can tell foundation models apart?

    I lead a study of whether Earth-observation benchmarks can reliably tell geospatial foundation models apart.

    Skills: Evaluation science · Benchmark reliability · Seed variance · Foundation models

  • Zero-shot VLM supervision · MIT · AAAI 2024

    A national crop map of Thailand from Street View and zero-shot VLM labels

    Zero-shot GPT‑4V labels of Street View photos supervise a national crop map, with no expert labels in training, and come close to expert supervision.

    Skills: Zero-shot VLM labelling · Pseudo-labelling · Street-to-satellite transfer · Satellite time series
