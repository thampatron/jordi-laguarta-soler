Microsoft Research · May – Aug 2024 · Research internship

TerraFlow: a multimodal foundation model for satellite time series

Self-supervised masked cross-modal pretraining on 20 TB of monthly radar, optical and land-cover imagery.

What I did

I designed the architecture for long multi-sensor time series: monthly Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 optical and Dynamic World land cover. Pretraining is self-supervised: months of one modality are masked and predicted from the others, a label-free cross-modal reconstruction objective. I built the 20 TB data pipeline and ran distributed multi-GPU pretraining and evaluation, for Microsoft Research's AI for Agriculture and AI for Industry teams.
