Microsoft Research · May – Aug 2024

TerraFlow: a multimodal foundation model for satellite time series

Self-supervised multimodal pretraining on 20 TB of monthly radar, optical and land-cover time series, with distributed multi-GPU training and high-throughput data streaming.

J. Laguarta Soler · research intern

Skills: Self-supervised pretraining · Cross-modal prediction · Distributed multi-GPU training · High-throughput data streaming

My role

I designed the encoder architecture, ran the distributed multi-GPU pretraining and built the 20 TB data pipeline, optimising its streaming throughput. I worked with Microsoft Research's AI for Agriculture and AI for Industry teams.

Problem

Crop and land-cover models are usually trained task by task from scarce ground labels. TerraFlow pretrains one encoder on radar, optical and land-cover time series with no ground labels, so downstream tasks can reuse its representations instead of training from scratch.

Method

The encoder takes long multimodal time series: monthly Sentinel-1 radar, Sentinel-2 optical and Dynamic World land-cover maps. Pretraining is self-supervised cross-modal prediction: the model predicts one modality from the other two.
