MIT Earth Intelligence Lab · 2026 – now

Which Earth-observation benchmarks can tell foundation models apart?

An analysis of published results that asks, benchmark by benchmark, whether reported gaps between geospatial foundation models are larger than seed noise.

J. Laguarta Soler · project lead

My role

I lead the project, with advice from Sherrie Wang and the lab. I built the dataset taxonomy, scoring pipeline and latent-factor analysis; the scoring follows a lab note by Sherrie Wang.

Problem

Geospatial foundation-model papers report dozens of benchmarks, usually from a single seed. Few test whether a one- or two-point gap between models exceeds seed noise.

Method

Each comparison is one paper's evaluation of several models on one benchmark. Its score is the share of the spread between models that exceeds seed noise, computed over all models and again over the stronger half, because weak baselines make almost any benchmark look reliable.

Status

Analysis in progress; findings are not yet public.
